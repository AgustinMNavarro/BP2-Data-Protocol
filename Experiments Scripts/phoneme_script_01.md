## Script

Early one morning, Sam grabbed his thick brown coat and stepped outside. The air was sharp and cold, yet the sky glowed with a soft yellow light. Birds sang joyfully through the trees while a gentle breeze swept the valley below.

Sam followed the winding path toward the old bridge. Just then, he heard a strange noise — something rustling in the bushes near the river. His heart raced. He breathed slowly, thinking it might be a fox or a deer.

Suddenly, a young girl appeared, her vision blurred from crying. "I've been lost for hours," she said. "Can you help me find my way home? I'm not sure which direction to go."

Sam smiled warmly. "Of course," he replied. "I know every trail here. Follow me — we'll be there before the sun sets."

Together, they walked through the golden afternoon, sharing stories, laughter, and the pleasure of each other's company.
