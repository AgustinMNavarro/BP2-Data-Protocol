# Protocol P1 (A) - Letters & Numbers

British English letters-and-numbers script for controlled prosody.
Use natural pacing, keep commas audible, and follow arrows exactly.

## Global rules
- Letters are spoken as letter names, not words.
- Keep a short pause at each comma, and a clear reset at full stops.
- Speak numbers in full words (for example, `1,204` as "one thousand, two hundred and four").
- Keep lexical focus on **bold** words; apply prosodic motion where arrows are attached.

## 1) ↘ Falling (neutral statement)
- We logged **A, J, Q, X, Z↘**, then filed **14↘**, **386↘**, and **1,204↓**.
- Please read **B, D, G, P, T↘**, then confirm **19↘**, **740↘**, and **12,500↓**.
- For the baseline, mark **C, F, K, L, R↘**, plus **8↘**, **905↘**, and **300,001↓**.

## 2) ↗ Rising (question)
- Could you repeat **E, H, M, N, Y↗**, then check **3↗**, **611↗**, and **2,010↑**?
- Shall we keep **I, O, U, W, V↗**, with **0↗**, **999↗**, and **50,600↑**?
- Do we store **S, T, X, Q, J↗**, beside **17↗**, **451↗**, and **7,000↑**?

## 3) ↗↘ Rise-Fall (contrastive emphasis)
- Not **five**, but **fifteen↗↘**, with **A, E, I, O, U↓**, and the tag **5,015↓**.
- Keep **Q and X↗↘**, not **K and S↓**, then attach **62↓**, **480↓**, and **900,300↓**.
- It was **Zed↗↘**, not **Zee↓**, before **11↓**, **704↓**, and **1,000,008↓**.

## 4) ↘↗ Fall-Rise (uncertain / polite)
- I think **B, V, F, TH, DH↘↗**, could pair with **6↘↗**, **570↘↗**, and **41,200↘↗**.
- Perhaps **CH, J, SH, ZH, NG↘↗**, fit better after **2↘↗**, **330↘↗**, and **600,040↘↗**.
- Maybe **R, L, W, Y, H↘↗**, should follow **10↘↗**, **812↘↗**, and **70,019↘↗**.

## 5) → Level (flat / procedural)
- Sequence **A, B, C, D, E→**, then **1→**, **21→**, **121→**, **1,121→**.
- Sequence **F, G, H, I, J→**, then **2→**, **32→**, **232→**, **2,232→**.
- Sequence **K, L, M, N, O→**, then **3→**, **43→**, **343→**, **3,343→**.

## 6) ↑ High Rise (surprised question)
- Wait, **Q, X, and Z↑**, with only **4↑**, **404↑**, and **4,004↑**?
- Really, **nought↑**, then **999↑**, then **9,999↑**?
- You heard **J, G, and CH↑**, before **13↑**, **713↑**, and **13,713↑**?

## 7) ↓ Low Fall (authoritative finality)
- Final entry: **A through Z↓**, with **20↓**, **820↓**, and **820,000↓**.
- Locked set: **Q, W, E, R, T↓**, then **18↓**, **618↓**, and **90,018↓**.
- Signed record: **Y, U, I, O, P↓**, plus **16↓**, **516↓**, and **1,516,000↓**.
