This script is designed for a **five-minute** practice session. It follows an adventure narrative through a mysterious landscape, targeting the full range of English phonemes — including consonant clusters, rare letters (Q, X, Z), fricatives, diphthongs, and pitch contours.

> **How to use:** Read each line aloud at a calm, measured pace. Pause briefly where marked _(Pause.)_ The four labelled drill sections are part of the script — read them naturally, as if the narrator is practising alongside you.
