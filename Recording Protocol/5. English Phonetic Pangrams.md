Set of unique pangrams designed to hit the extremes of the English vowel quadrilateral and consonant spectrum.

Read this 3 times:
- different speeds (normal, fast, slow)
- different tempo
- different pitch/tone (whisper, high)

---

### The "Vowel Vault" (The Resonance Test)

_Focus: Long vowels, diphthongs, and mouth-opening extremes (High-Front to Low-Back)._

> **"A huge, grey owl flew through the bright blue sky to view the calm, green marsh below."** _(Covers: /uː/, /eɪ/, /aʊ/, /aɪ/, /iː/, and /ɑː/)_

### The "Articulator" (The Technical Test)

_Focus: All 26 letters plus tricky British English clusters like 'th' and 'gh'._

> **"The beige vulture quickly snatched a quartz gem from the zephyr's path, though he thought of joys below."** _(Covers: Every letter, voiced/unvoiced 'th', and the 'zh' /ʒ/ sound)_

### The "Sibilant Stream" (The Texture Test)

_Focus: Fricatives, affricates, and nasals to test "ess" sounds and breath control._

> **"Six short, charming chefs sang songs whilst mashing fresh, salty fish in the kitchen."** _(Focus: /s/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/, /ŋ/, and /f/)_
