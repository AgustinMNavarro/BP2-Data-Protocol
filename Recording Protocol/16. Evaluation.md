## Purpose
Use this evaluation recording to check whether the current model is converging or diverging.
The key test is simple: given an evaluation clip, can the trained model convert the source voice into the target speaker’s voice while preserving intelligibility and natural flow.

## When to Record
Record one evaluation sample at the end of each training epoch (or at your defined evaluation interval).

## Required Format
- Duration: about **70 seconds** of continuous speech.
- Content: **general speech only** (random topic, do not use sensitive or overly specific personal details).
- Voice modes in this exact order:
1. **Normal↓**
2. **Whisper→**
3. **Projected↑**
- Delivery: smooth speech, stable pace, clear transitions between modes.

## Recording Steps
1. Start in a neutral speaking pace and tone.
2. Speak in **normal** voice for the first segment.
3. Transition clearly to **whisper** and continue the same topic.
4. Transition clearly to **projected** (louder, supported voice; avoid shouting distortion).
5. Keep all three segments in one continuous recording.

## Suggested Script Template

> This is a **guide only** — adapt the words to sound natural for you. The goal is one continuous recording of about **70 seconds**, moving smoothly through three voice modes without stopping.

---

_(Start recording, then begin speaking.)_

"Hi, my name is [Name]. I'm going to talk about [choose any everyday topic — a walk, a meal, the weather] in three different voice styles."

---

**1. Normal voice** _(relaxed, conversational — your everyday speaking tone)_

"In my normal voice, I'll describe something from this morning. I woke up a little earlier than usual, made a warm drink, and sat by the window for a while. The street outside was quiet — a few people passing, a distant engine, the sound of a door closing somewhere nearby. It's the kind of slow start that makes the rest of the day feel a bit more manageable."

---

**2. Transition to whisper** _(shift smoothly — no pause, no gap in the recording)_

> Say this out loud as part of the script — it signals the mode change naturally.

"Now I'll carry on in a **softer→** voice — same pace, same story, just quieter."

**Whisper** _(keep every syllable clear and shaped; avoid hollow breathiness)_

"Still on the same morning. I stepped outside for a moment, heard birds in a tree nearby, and noticed someone arranging things in a shop window. Even quietly, every word stays deliberate and easy to follow. A whisper should feel controlled, not strained."

---

**3. Transition to projected** _(shift smoothly — no pause, no gap in the recording)_

> Say this out loud as part of the script — it signals the mode change naturally.

"And now, **projected↑** — more energy, same steady pace."

**Projected** _(use full breath support; carry the voice forward without shouting)_

"In projected mode, I finish the story with more presence. The afternoon was straightforward — a short walk, a quiet meal, a few pages of reading before the light faded. My voice carries further now, but the rhythm stays the same. Clear consonants, open vowels, and consistent timing, right through to the very last word."

---

_(Stop recording.)_

## QA Checklist
- [ ] Clip length is close to 70 seconds.
- [ ] Sequence is **normal → whisper → projected**.
- [ ] Speech is continuous and smooth.
- [ ] No clipping, severe noise, or abrupt gain changes.
- [ ] Topic remains general and non-sensitive.

## Language Note
English is preferred.
If the collection language is not English, also record one evaluation clip in the collected language.
