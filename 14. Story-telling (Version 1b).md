This script is designed for a **five-minute** practice session. It travels across the European continent, blending royal history, diverse geography, and phonetic challenges to cover every sound in the English language, including the rare pangram letters (J, Q, X, Z).

> **How to use:** Read each line aloud at a calm, measured pace. Pause briefly where marked _(Pause.)_ Give royal names and place lists a slightly grander, more deliberate delivery.

---
