> **Delivery:** Start at a comfortable, confident pitch and let your voice fall steadily toward the end. The last word lands **low and final** — like closing a door. No question, no hesitation.

> **Delivery:** Let your voice fall in the middle — as if making a cautious point — then **gently rise at the end**. It sounds like you are leaving the door open, inviting agreement rather than asserting a fact.
> **Example:** "Perhaps we should wait until tomorrow before making any kind of **decision↘↗**."

> **Delivery:** Keep your pitch completely **even from start to finish** — no rise, no fall, no emotion. Every word gets the same weight. Imagine reading a list of facts to a machine.

## ↘↘ strong fall (authoritative finality)

> **Delivery:** Every clause drops firmly. **No hesitation, no softening, no upward turn anywhere.** It should sound like a decision that has already been made and will not be changed.
> **Example:** "You **must↘** submit the full report before the office closes this **afternoon↓**!"
