## **Pitch markers, qualities, non‑lexicals, domains**

1. **Pitch markers:** `[Normal]`, `[Question]`, `[Exclaim]` at start.
2. **Voice qualities:** whisper, shout, sigh, laugh, mutter, calm, urgency, frustration, excitement.
3. **Non‑lexicals:** Hmm, Mmmm, Ah, Ahha, Ya, Haha at sentence start.
4. **Global names & places:** UK, US, EU, Asia, Africa, Middle East, Americas, Southeast Asia, Scandinavia.
5. **Airlines:** Emirates, Delta, KLM, SAS, ANA, Qatar Airways, Aer Lingus, British Airways, Qantas, Lufthansa, Singapore Airlines.
6. **Weather & holiday context:** drizzle, blizzard, monsoon, typhoon, Ramadan, Diwali, Christmas, Eid, Carnival, Lunar New Year, winter, snow, rainy, frost, spring.
7. **Emotions:** urgency, calm, frustration, excitement, wonder, joy.
