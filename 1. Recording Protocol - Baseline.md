### Phase 1: Preparation (Buffer)

- **Initial Silence (5 seconds):** Stay still and quiet — this captures the room’s background noise for later cleanup.

---
### Phase 2: Identification & Context

- **Greeting and Purpose:**
  > "Hi, this is [Full Name]. I’m recording my voice baseline on [Day], [Date Month Year], at around [Time]."

- **Location and Environment:**
  > "I’m recording in [e.g., my bedroom / a quiet room]. The room is [silent / has some background hum]. Outside it’s [e.g., overcast, about 18 degrees]."

- **Setup:**
  > "I’m [sitting / standing], about [e.g., ten centimetres] from the mic."

- **Physical State:**
  > "I’m feeling [e.g., well-rested]. No sore throat, no allergies — voice should be clear."

- **Consent:**
  > "I’m happy for this recording to be used for research."

---

### Phase 3: Linguistic & Phonetic Range

- **The Pangram:**
  > "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog."

- **Vowel Extension** — hold each sound steadily for about 5 seconds at a comfortable speaking pitch:

| Vowel | Example word | Say...  |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| /ɑː/  | f**a**ther   | "Ahhh…" |
| /iː/  | s**ee**      | "Eeee…" |
| /ɒ/   | l**o**t      | "Ohh…"  |
| /uː/  | b**oo**t     | "Ooo…"  |

  > _Aim for a steady, even tone — not singing, not straining. Just hold it like you’re saying "ahh" at the doctor._

---

### Phase 4: Dynamic Range (Volume & Tone)

- **Whisper**
  > "This is my whisper — keeping it quiet, but still making each word clear."

- **Natural / Modal**
  > "This is my normal speaking voice — relaxed, like chatting with a friend."

- **Projected**
  > "This is my projected voice — like calling across a room to get someone’s attention."

---

### Phase 5: Conclusion (Buffer)

- **Final Silence (5 seconds):** Stay still — provides a clean ending to the recording.
